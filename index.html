🏆 USER MANUAL

1. THE CORE

  • Scramble & Solve: Look at the top scramble, solve ur cube, and enter ur time!
  • Ao5 Set: Repeat 5 times to complete an Average of 5 (Ao5) set.
  • Live Bot Lobby: Your final Ao5 is automatically pinned against 23 live bots!
  • Baseline Target: Beat your dynamic Baseline time to gain Versus Rating (VR).
  • Rating Drop: If your Ao5 falls behind the baseline, your rating will DROP!
  • Rank Titles: Color and rank title change as your VR rises up to 9999 VR.
  • Secret Layout: Reaching 9999 unlocks a secret animated custom layout effect!

2. MULTI-EVENT TRACKING

  • 4 Supported Events: Toggle between 3x3, 2x2, 4x4, and OH.
  • Independent Stats: Each event tracks its own separate VR, peak history, and podiums.

3. +2 & DNFs

  • Oh No, a Bad Solve?: Tap any display time slot to cycle through penalties:
  • 1st Click: Applies a +2s Penalty
  • 2nd Click: Applies a DNF (Did Not Finish)
  • 3rd Click: Resets back to your Normal original time.
  • ⚠️ Warning: a DNF Ao5 will cause an INSTANT -200 VR LOSS!!!

4. FUN MODE

  • Skip the Grind: Don't have time to climb the all that? Use the sandbox mode!
  • How to Toggle: Tap the [MODE: CUBE] badge to switch to [MODE: FUN].
  • Custom Rating: Directly type any value (1 - 9999 VR) to preview the 9999 [UNREAL] master layout!

5. TIMER MODE

  • An In-game Timer: Don't have a timer with you? Use the timer model!
  • How to Toggle: Tap the [MODE: CUBE] badge to switch to [MODE: TIMER].
  • Time Your Solves: Press anywhere to start a 15s inspection. Press again to start the timer. Press again to stop the timer.

6. BACKGROUND WALLPAPERS

  • Lock in with Waifus: Scroll to the very bottom to customize your backdrop.
  • Gallery Upload: Tap "Select Photos from Gallery" to insert your favorite anime character!
  • Note: The middle portion of your photo will be layered behind the main grid panel.

  • Don't Rage Quit: Do not quit mid-Ao5 even if ur first few solves went horribly. Overcoming bad rounds and accepting the VR loss is exactly how u improve.
  • 👉 TRUST THE PROCESS!!!
